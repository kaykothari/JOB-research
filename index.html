        Macro Economics Brief

        Comparing raw dollar figures across countries doesn't tell the whole story. While the US offers the highest raw USD salaries, your real take-home quality of life differs dramatically based on healthcare, working hours, and local living costs:

        USA • High Earning, High Out-of-Pocket
        High gross pay ($130k+), but private health insurance deductibles cost $6k–$12k/year, housing in tech hubs (SF/NYC) is extreme, and immigration is "at-will" (loss of job = 60 days to leave). Highest potential to rapidly pay off debt and accumulate equity.
        AUS / NZ • Free Healthcare & +11.5% Super
        While 1 AUD ≈ $0.65 USD, universal healthcare (Medicare) is free, companies legally pay +11.5% superannuation (pension) on top of base pay, workweeks are capped at 38 hours, and 4 weeks paid vacation is statutory. High daily buying power.
        Canada • Universal Care & Fast PR Safety
        While 1 CAD ≈ $0.73 USD, provincial healthcare is free and securing PR within 2–3 years eliminates visa panic. Once Canadian citizenship is granted, professionals can use the non-lottery TN visa to work in the US for full US rates.

        Primary SkillBadgeThe main daily technical or strategic capability you need to excel (e.g. Coding, Real-Time Rendering, Cloud Infrastructure).
